# Prop ownership

Own only your assigned prop folder and local resources. Place each prefab root at
the origin. Save colliders, Hotspot children, local Approach Marker3D and editable
materials with the prefab. Solid bodies persist in nav_source; tiny held props do not.
Hotspot exports visual: Node3D and approach_marker: Marker3D; references are local.
Location instances own placement and may override puzzle prose/settings.
